# Credits

HealthScan is an original app. Its code, scoring rules, icon, colors and wording were
written for this project; no code, artwork or text from any other scanner app is used.

## Libraries
- **ZXing core 3.5.3** (barcode reading) — Apache License 2.0,
  https://github.com/zxing/zxing. Pulled from Maven Central by Gradle
  (`com.google.zxing:core:3.5.3`).
- **JUnit 4.13.2** (unit tests only, not shipped in the app) — Eclipse Public License 1.0.

## Product data
- **Open Food Facts** and **Open Beauty Facts** — open databases, data under the
  Open Database License (ODbL). Looked up by barcode only when you scan or search.

Product photos are loaded from Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts and are licensed by their
contributors under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA). The README
